<idea>
**Design Movement**: Deep Space Brutalism — raw, technical, observatory-grade
**Core Principles**:
- Monochromatic near-black palette with single electric accent (nebula cyan)
- Stark typographic contrast: ultra-heavy display weight vs. light body
- Data-forward layouts: metadata, coordinates, exposure data treated as design elements
- Uncompromising dark-first approach — the sky is always dark

**Color Philosophy**: Near-black (#0a0c10) base with deep navy undertones. Single accent: ionized oxygen teal (#4ECDC4). Secondary: hydrogen-alpha red (#C0392B) for alerts/highlights. Rationale: mimics the actual color channels used in narrowband astrophotography.

**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric editorial grid — hero images bleed full-width, text blocks anchored left with generous right-side negative space. Gallery uses masonry with variable-height cards.

**Signature Elements**:
- Thin hairline rules (1px) separating sections, like coordinate grid lines
- Monospaced metadata overlays on images (RA/Dec, exposure time, date)
- Star-field particle canvas as subtle background texture

**Interaction Philosophy**: Precision over playfulness. Hover states reveal hidden data layers. Transitions are quick and purposeful (150-200ms).

**Animation**: Subtle fade-in on scroll with slight upward drift. Image hover: scale 1.02 with metadata overlay sliding up. No bouncy or elastic animations.

**Typography System**: Display: Space Grotesk Bold (900) for headings. Body: DM Sans (400/500) for readability. Mono: JetBrains Mono for technical data/metadata.
</idea>

- Rich deep navy/indigo backgrounds with warm gold accents
- Serif display typography evoking scientific journals and star atlases

**Layout Paradigm**: Magazine-style feature layout. Hero sections use full-bleed imagery with overlaid text. Gallery uses a curated editorial grid, not uniform masonry.

**Signature Elements**:
- Thin gold horizontal rules as section dividers
- Circular crop thumbnails for "featured" items
- Constellation-dot decorative patterns in section backgrounds

**Interaction Philosophy**: Deliberate and immersive. Smooth scroll-triggered reveals. Gallery lightbox with cinematic fade transition.

**Animation**: Scroll-triggered entrance animations with 300ms ease-out. Image hover: warm golden border glow. Navigation: smooth underline draw on hover.

**Typography System**: Display: Playfair Display (700/900) for headings. Body: Source Serif 4 (400) for long-form text. Labels: Montserrat (500, tracked) for navigation and metadata.

- Pure black background, white typography, single accent color

**Signature Elements**:
- Large typographic section numbers (01, 02, 03) as visual anchors
- Thin dashed borders on image cards suggesting measurement/analysis
- Subtle noise texture overlay on dark sections

**Interaction Philosophy**: Invisible UI that reveals itself on demand. Hover states are the primary discovery mechanism.

**Animation**: Minimal. Opacity transitions only. Gallery images: slight desaturation at rest, full color on hover.

**Typography System**: Display: Bebas Neue for large headings. Body: Inter (400/500). Captions: IBM Plex Mono for technical data.

## Selected Approach: Cinematic Observatory

Chosen for its balance of luxury and scientific credibility. The deep navy/gold palette honors the tradition of star atlas cartography while feeling modern and premium. Playfair Display brings editorial gravitas appropriate for long-form astrophotography storytelling.
